ABOUT JAN SETU

A bridge from community needs to practical solutions.

Citizens report real problems. JAN SETU helps structure, match and move them toward collaborative action.

One connected problem-solving journey

JAN SETU brings citizens, students, researchers and industry or innovation partners into one clear workflow. Reports stay grounded in the information provided by the citizen, while solvers receive structured problems that are easier to understand and act on.
